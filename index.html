The Alliance Rule

Never farm a non-farming player or alliance. Everyone else is fair game. Expect to get farmed and expect to fight back.

  • Before you hit anyone, scout them first.
  • If they do not farm others, leave them alone.
  • If they farm, they are fair game. Expect them to come back at us.

Our Job

VHU was made to have fun, grow, and take the fight to others. We attack enemy HQs and the "Tanks" (enemy marches that come out of their HQs to attack us).

Fun

Play together, help each other, laugh a lot.

Grow

Help every member get stronger. Bigger HQ, bigger power, bigger wins.

Fight

Rally together. One caller. Hit weak spots. Protect our own.

Handling "Tanks"

Tanks are enemy marches that come out of HQs to attack us. They are our biggest danger and our best chance.

  • Call it out fast. Post who is being hit, where, and how many troops are coming.
  • Reinforce. Send troops into the member being hit so the attackers face a bigger wall.
  • Counter-rally. An HQ that just sent its troops out is weaker. Rally it with the strongest players.
  • Protect the small. Newer and weaker members should shield up.
  • Keep hospitals empty and use shields when we are under heavy attack.
  • Counter the faction. Wings beat Blood Rose. Blood Rose beat Guard of Order. Guard of Order beat Wings.

Peace Day

Do not attack anyone. Only plunder your selected server.

  • Heal your troops and refill your hospital space.
  • Upgrade: HQ, heroes, tech.
  • Scout targets for the next attack day. Write down who farms and who does not.
  • Help newer members grow.
  • Pick the caller and the targets for the next fight.

Saturday: Alliance Duel Combat Day

Saturday is the best attack day. It is the combat day of the Alliance Duel (Day 6). You can teleport to the enemy server for free, and every kill gives points (4 points per kill).

  • Everyone online, rested, and healed before the day starts.
  • One caller. One plan. Rally the strong targets.
  • Use a 24-hour shield if we are winning and want to protect the lead.
  • A duel needs about 20 or more members, so every member counts. CHECK IN GAME

Volcano War

Volcano War is a 10-day event between servers. The fight happens on Day 6 at 12:00 Apocalypse Time for exactly 1 hour.

  • You need HQ Level 15 or higher to teleport in.
  • You need 2,000 personal points to get ranking rewards.
  • The core zone has no teleport and no shield.
  • Higher-value spots give more points per second (Black Ridge Volcano is the top spot).

Apocalypse Time = Central Time + 4 hours. These numbers come from one guide, so please check them in game. VERIFY
